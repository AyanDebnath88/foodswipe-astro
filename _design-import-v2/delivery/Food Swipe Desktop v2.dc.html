Food Swipe · design language v2 · desktop 1280–1600
Wide screens get more room, not bigger buttons.
Desktop keeps the full-bleed photography and the ink-pill CTA, then spends its width on context: a persistent room rail, live vote state, and a hero that runs to the viewport edge. The swipe card is still capped — drag ergonomics don't scale with a monitor.
